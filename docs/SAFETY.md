# Safety

- **Power.** Use a low-voltage DC supply in an enclosure, with a fuse. Exposed boards sit under a cover. The off switch is within reach of the operator.
- **Hands.** Switch the sound off before placing beads or reaching into the levitator.
- **Sound.** 40 kHz is above human hearing, so the levitator seems quiet even while it is running. Nobody has measured this build's ultrasonic or audible levels yet. Measure them with a meter before long sessions or a public showing.
- **People and animals.** Pets, children and spectators stay back from a running levitator until those levels are measured. The exhibit works from a screen, so nobody needs to lean in.
- **Mounting.** Fix both transducer arrays firmly to the frame, and set the frame on a heavy base that does not wobble.
